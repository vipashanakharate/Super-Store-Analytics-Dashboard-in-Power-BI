🧠 Superstore Analytics Dashboard – Power BI Project
📊 Overview
This Power BI project visualizes and analyzes sales performance for a retail superstore dataset. The Superstore Analytics Dashboard provides a clear overview of key business metrics such as sales, profit, and returned orders, along with deeper insights into performance by product, region, and customer segment.
The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders quickly identify growth trends, regional performance, and product profitability to make data-driven decisions.
________________________________________
🚀 Features
•	KPI Cards:
o	Total Sales ($2.33M)
o	Total Profit ($292.3K)
o	% Returned Orders (5.79%)
o	Year-over-Year comparisons with growth rates
•	Trend Analysis:
o	Sales vs. Previous Year displayed over time for clear trend visualization
•	Profit Breakdown:
o	Profit by Product Category (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology)
o	Highlighting profitable and underperforming product lines
•	Regional Insights:
o	Profit by Directional Region (West, East, Central, South)
•	Customer Segmentation:
o	Sales distribution across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments
________________________________________
🛠️ Tools & Technologies
•	Power BI Desktop – for data modeling and visualization
•	Data Source – Superstore dataset (sample retail dataset)
•	DAX – for calculated measures and KPIs
•	Power Query – for data cleaning and transformation
________________________________________
📈 Key Insights
•	Sales increased 47.16% compared to the previous year.
•	Profit rose 48.85%, showing strong financial growth.
•	Returned orders decreased by 2.95%, indicating improved customer satisfaction.
•	The Technology category led in profitability, while Tables underperformed.
•	The West region generated the highest profit.
•	The Consumer segment accounted for over 50% of total sales.
